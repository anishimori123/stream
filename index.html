              How to get J.PERIOD's free mixtapes onto your phone
              1. Open his official Bandcamp or SoundCloud link above on your Pixel.
              2. Pick a mixtape that says Free Download or name your price (you can enter 0).
              3. Download it. If you get a .zip file, open the Files app, tap the zip, then tap Extract.
              4. Come back here, tap ➕ Add audio file, and pick the mp3 file(s).
              5. Done! It now plays offline. You can delete the original file from Downloads to save space.

              Only download from his official pages. If a mixtape has a price, buying it supports the artist.
